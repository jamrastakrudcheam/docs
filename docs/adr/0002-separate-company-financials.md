# ICC and ITK financials stay separate; no consolidation

ICC and ITK each have their own Company, Legal Entity and Primary Ledger, with several OUs under scope grants (D05, P11). Every report (TB, balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, equity) is forced to one company/ledger, and the first release has no consolidation or intercompany elimination (D14), even though the Blueprint described consolidated statements. Mixing ICC and ITK figures in any screen or export is a defect, not a feature gap.
